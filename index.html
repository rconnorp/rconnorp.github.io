BYU Sunoco Case Competition · March 2026 · 1st place of ~30 teams

Sunoco's acquisition of Offen Petroleum

We proposed that Sunoco acquire Offen Petroleum to close a six-state distribution gap in PADD IV, a region Sunoco's NuStar infrastructure didn't cover. Our model showed $180M in combined EBITDA ($110M standalone plus $70M in synergies) at an effective 5.1x post-synergy multiple, with more than 10% DCF accretion by Year 3 and roughly a 19% IRR. We financed it with $400M in cash, a $375M term loan, and a $150M revolver. That kept net leverage at 4.1x, under Sunoco's 5.0x covenant.
